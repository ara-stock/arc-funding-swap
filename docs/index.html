How it works

Three steps, one rule

One side pays a fixed rate and receives the actual funding; the other side does the opposite. Over the term, whoever was right gets paid from the other's deposit.

1

Post an offer

Pick a side, a fixed rate per day, a size (notional) and a term. Your deposit (margin) is locked in the contract.

2

Someone takes it

A counterparty locks the same deposit. The swap starts at the next UTC midnight and runs for whole days.

3

Automatic settlement

At maturity anyone can trigger settlement. The difference between actual and fixed funding moves to the winner, capped at the deposit.

Worked example

Alice pays fixed0.025% per day · deposits 10 USDC
⇄ 7 days on 1,000 USDC
Bob receives fixedpays actual funding · deposits 10 USDC
BTC funding actually averages 0.035%/day. Over 7 days the actual leg is 1,000 × 0.245% = 2.45 USDC and the fixed leg is 1,000 × 0.175% = 1.75 USDC. Alice receives the difference, 0.70 USDC, from Bob's deposit. If funding had averaged 0.015%/day, Bob would receive 0.70 USDC instead.

Basis traders: lock your yield

Funding views: express them directly

The funding index

  • Every value is the sum of Hyperliquid's public hourly funding for that day. Anyone can recompute it (rule).
  • Append-only: a day can be posted once, only after it ends, and never changed.
  • If a maturity value is not posted within 7 days, both sides can reclaim their full deposit.
Experimental and unaudited. Deposits are capped at 100 USDC per side and terms at 30 days. The index currently has a single publisher. Only use amounts you can afford to lose.
