Perp funding is a fee that changes every hour. Fix it for a week.

Anyone holding a Hyperliquid perp pays or earns funding, a fee that resets every hour and can double or vanish in days. Funding Swap lets you swap that moving fee for one fixed rate on HYPE, SOL, DOGE or BTC for 1 to 30 days. The other side is a trader who thinks the fee will move your way. Both deposit USDC on Arc; the contract pays the difference to whoever was right.
